FotoBrowser › FotoBrowser vs Apple Photos

A free Apple Photos alternative for Mac that works with your own folders

Apple Photos keeps your pictures inside one Photos library. FotoBrowser leaves them as ordinary files in your own folders — on your Mac, on external drives and on backup drives — and gives you a Photos-style way to browse, organise and edit them. It's free, runs entirely on your Mac, and doesn't need iCloud.

Who it's for

  • You keep your photos in folders, or spread across several external and backup drives.
  • You shoot RAW and want your originals never rewritten.
  • You'd rather not keep everything inside one library file, or in iCloud.
  • You want duplicates found across all your folders and drives, not just inside one library.

Moving from Apple Photos

  1. In Photos, select the photos you want (⌘A for all), choose File ▸ Export ▸ Export Unmodified Originals, and pick a folder.
  2. In FotoBrowser, click ＋ in the sidebar and add that folder, or drag the folder onto the sidebar.
  3. FotoBrowser indexes it and reads the dates, locations and keywords that are in the files. Nothing is removed from Photos.

Questions

Can I use FotoBrowser and Apple Photos at the same time?

Yes. FotoBrowser never opens or changes your Photos library, so both can run side by side: keep Photos for iCloud and your iPhone, and use FotoBrowser for the photos you keep in folders and on drives.

Can FotoBrowser open my Apple Photos library?

No. It doesn't read inside a .photoslibrary. Export the photos from Photos into a folder (File ▸ Export ▸ Export Unmodified Originals) and add that folder to FotoBrowser.

Do my Apple Photos edits, albums and people come across?

Not automatically. Unmodified originals don't include edits made in Photos (export the edited versions instead if you want them baked in), and albums and named people stay in Photos. FotoBrowser groups faces again on your Mac, and you can rebuild albums and favourites.

Does FotoBrowser sync with iCloud Photos?

No. Your photos stay on your Mac's drives. To bring in photos from an iPhone, connect it with a cable: FotoBrowser copies new photos into a folder you choose and never deletes them from the phone.

What does Apple Photos do that FotoBrowser doesn't?

iCloud Photos sync, apps for iPhone and iPad, shared albums, and Memories movies. FotoBrowser is a Mac app for the photos stored on your Mac and its drives.

Is FotoBrowser open source?

No. It's free to download, but its source code isn't public.
